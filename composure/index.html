The Collaborator

An agent in the band.
Two ways in.

Most AI music tools hand you back a file. Composure's agent reaches the engine itself, so a request lands on the live performance — transpose the bass, drop the drums, build a patch, shift the mood. Everything else keeps playing.

In the app

Ask it to play, sketch, or change what's playing. It happens in the window in front of you.

Outside the app

Flip one switch and Substrate sees Composure as a connected instrument and drives it from there.

Dungeon Master

Give it a brief — a progression, a difficulty, a skill to practice. It turns practice into a scene.

Brainstorm

Describe the idea in words and it exists — a part, a kit, a patch. The prompt is the sketch.

“An agent that can only read the song is an assistant. One that can change a single voice without disturbing the others is a bandmate.”

In Your DAW

It shows up in your DAW
like any other instrument.

Play from the app, hear it through the sounds you already use — Ableton, Logic, FL Studio, Maschine, any of them.

  • —In time, either way — it can set the tempo, or follow yours.
  • —Follows your hands — sustain, and touch that bends as you move.
  • —Your keyboard, remembered — plug it in once and it's there next time.

Notation

Play it or hum it.
Get sheet music.

Switch to Composer and what you played is written out as real notation — key signatures, tied notes, treble and bass — not a piano roll. Export it as a clean image for a chart or a lead sheet, dark or light.

Sound

Every sound can
become an instrument.

Four short doors into the same engine. Take whichever one is closest to the idea in your head.

Hum it in

Sing or hum a line and it becomes notes you can edit, right on the device. Offline — nothing leaves the machine.

Record it

Grab a sound from the mic or from your computer, trim it, and build a playable instrument around it.

Describe it

Tell the agent the sound you want and it builds the patch.

Generate it

Soundscapes writes ambient music straight onto your channels. Pick a mood, the whole band answers, and point any part at any voice you like.

Warm Dark Mystic Deep Calm Sad Tense Sweet Cinematic Ethereal Cosmic Meditative Joy Dreamlike

Synth Forge is a full workbench — oscillators, filters, envelopes — and it draws the sound as standing-wave patterns while you tune.

Radio is a map of stations with a live 3D view, and it can generate music as it plays, steered by a word from you.

Pocket and Desk

In your pocket,
on your desk.

The Android build is the full instrument, not a viewer.

  • —Standalone — the whole thing runs on the phone or tablet.
  • —Controller — on the same network it plays the desktop, so the tablet on your stand is an input surface for the instrument at the desk.
  • —Feels like playing — pads and keys answer with a tap back.
