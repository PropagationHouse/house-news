By Propagation HouseDesigner & DeveloperEst. 2013 · Bellingham, WA

Michael Blackstone is an independent designer & developer operating across dimensions: creative direction, brand systems, 2D & 3D visual design + full-stack web & cross-platform application development.

What the studio is

In 2013, he established Propagation House: an independent multimedia house, design studio & think tank. Over more than a decade of active practice, the work has spanned foundational graphic + web design & advertising campaigns, virtual architecture, proprietary software, a 260+ page book, & live 3D WebGL commercial environments serving hundreds of thousands, sometimes millions of users.

Selected work

Three engagements anchor the record. A three-part shoppable game series for Walmart's Unlimited catalog — playable 3D worlds where every item is a live SKU with instant zero-install checkout, 5.4% conversion across 1M+ total campaign users, press in Inc., AdAge & Retail TouchPoints. A reconstruction of the UN General Assembly Hall for COP30, commissioned via UNDP Brazil — built floor by floor in virtual 3D WebGL as an international space for press, educators & delegates, with real-time 7-language AI translation streams. And Substrate, the house's core platform: agentic design & development software that turns a machine from an inanimate silo into something conversational, proactive & autonomous — model-independent, runs offline or paired with any model online, ships on desktop & Android.

He also worked directly with Spatial's dev team & leadership as both creative collaborator & contracted hire: real-time multiplayer networking, interactive 3D spatial commerce pipelines, live SKU checkout hooks & zero-install cross-platform performance.

Operating & shipping

End-to-end delivery: brief → prototype → design → code → ship. Direct client interface — briefing, scoping, delivery & support, no account layer. Editorial & publishing: a 260+ page book, daily web publication, weekly newsletter. Production channels: print (ISBN/POD), live web, desktop & mobile apps. Technical documentation & specification writing. Civic parks project advocacy.
