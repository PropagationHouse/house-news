Composure: The Agent That Thinks in Channels, Not Tracks

A MIDI engine that refuses the timeline metaphor — and a sketchbook that refuses to choose between the studio and the stage. Inside the five-channel architecture where every event carries its own routing.

We didn’t want a model that produces songs. We wanted a model that holds a song in its head and lets you talk to the parts of it. The protocol is simple: every MIDI event carries a channel. The channel is the instrument. The instrument is the voice. You don’t arrange on a timeline. You address the room. Drums on one, lead on two, melody on three, ambience on four, bass on five. Save commits state instead of regenerating. The sketchbook remembers what you said, not what it heard.
