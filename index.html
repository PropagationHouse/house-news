The Proof Compiles

Claude rebuilt the proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem in the Lean proof assistant — 13 million lines, roughly 29,500 verified lemmas, eleven days, six billion tokens — completing the first machine-checked verification of the 1995 result, and beating the community formalization project that had scheduled the work for 2029.

Flagship · AI Agent

Substrate

Not a chatbot bolted onto a page — a home your agent lives in. Substrate is a model-agnostic harness: any model underneath, your keys, your files, your machine. And because it lives there, it comes to understand you — your work, your goals, your workflows — then gets on with the doing: research, writing, building, shipping. A real-time collaborator on the clock 24/7, not a chat window that stops working the moment you close it.

Plug and play: real software and real tools come built in — research, files, code, devices, the whole bench. Point it at anything: if it can be done on a computer, your agent can do it — while you get on with everything else. Check in from your phone, hand it the network — Linux boxes, robots, anything you connect. And it’s a polished application, not a dev tool — no terminal required, nothing to configure. You don’t have to be a developer to live with it; it just works.

Substrate is the layer everything is built upon. Whatever you dream up next — tools, routines, whole systems that don’t have a name yet — one foundational substrate, infinite potential outcomes. You are what makes your agent truly unique.
